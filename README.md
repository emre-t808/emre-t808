## I lead people and culture teams at scale-ups, and I research and build things for with this mission.

I lead People, Culture and Happiness at AppSamurai in London. I also design and build to make the most with the least. This includes custom job specific coding harnesses, bots, dasboards, agents and more.

I’m most at home when things are changing and there’s no obvious way forward. The aim is to find our footing, try things out and learn what works. My mental toolset is first principles and curiousity about how other fields solve problems, and what we might borrow and adapt from them.

I care about helping scale-ups do more with what they have, without wearing people down. Increasingly, that means building software that AI agents can use, and working out where they can make life easier for people. 

Smaller teams, faster teams.

### What I’m working on in the evenings

**[Harness Harness](https://github.com/emre-t808/harness-harness)**

Coding agents need guidance. I wanted to understand whether they actually use it. Harness Harness tracks which rules Claude Code follows and proposes changes for me to review. The source is available, it’s published on npm, and I use it and improve it every day.

**[Culture for People and Agents](https://github.com/emre-t808/people-agents-and-culture/blob/main/AX-DESIGNER.md)**

For example, what would we design differently if an AI agent were using our software? This is my attempt to explore that question through Agent Experience (AX): sixteen principles and a job description that others can adapt.

**Inside AppSamurai**

Most of what I build lives in private company repositories. It includes a platform that gives employees their own AI agent, with memory and company context.

Our People team uses these agents in its daily work, with a person approving anything before it is sent or changed. Finance, Legal and Customer Success now use the same approach with their own agents.
